Frontend and Data decide where. **Update 2026-11-14:** **Smooth 25 fps option (2026-11-14, Aadhitya):** the original Cholec80 videos are a public CC BY-NC-SA download with no request form, at `https://s3.unistra.fr/camma_public/datasets/cholec80/cholec80.zip` (74.9 GB). Listing verified: `videos/video01.mp4` is 692.7 MB, and the zip also has `videoNN-timestamp.txt`, `tool_annotations/`, and `phase_annotations/`. Matching CholecT50 frames against the video at 5 points per case gave constant offsets with no drift: VID01 -0.04 s (N=10, 680, 1700) and VID02 +0.08 s (N=100, 1000, 2000, 2800), at 42–45 dB PSNR.
