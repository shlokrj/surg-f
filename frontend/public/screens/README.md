# Screens

`model-step1.jpg` to `model-step3.jpg` are captures of Surg+F's own 3D model panel (case page, `/model`) at three steps of VID01: Calot's triangle dissection, clipping and cutting, and gallbladder dissection. They are used by the solution's "Practice in 3D" chapter on the presentation and video demo homepages.

The anatomy in them is built from **BodyParts3D**, © The Database Center for Life Science, licensed under CC Attribution 4.0 International. See [../models/ATTRIBUTION.md](../models/ATTRIBUTION.md) for the source and what was changed. The cystic artery, clips, cut and triangle are schematic drawings, not yet medically reviewed.

Recapture after changing the model: open a case, enter `/model`, step with the arrows, and screenshot `#model-stage`.
